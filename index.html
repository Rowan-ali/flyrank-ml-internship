Ranking Content at Risk of Becoming went_dark Using February Search Signals

FlyRank ML Internship — Capstone Research Project

Author: Rowan Ali

Model: Logistic Regression

Primary Metric: Precision@50 = 0.280

Validation ROC-AUC: 0.775

Abstract

This project develops a ranking model that identifies eligible content with an elevated risk of becoming went_dark in the following month. Predictive features were derived from February 2026 search and engagement signals, while March 2026 data were used exclusively for outcome construction. A Logistic Regression model with client-grouped validation achieved a Precision@50 of 0.280 and a ROC-AUC of 0.775. The resulting ranking is intended as a decision-support tool for prioritizing manual content review rather than establishing causal relationships.
